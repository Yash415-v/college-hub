# Yash's College Hub & Portfolio

A modern, responsive, and 100% free website to showcase your personal profile, track what you are currently learning, and share college notes, assignments, and lab manuals.

---

## 🚀 How to Preview Locally (On Your PC)
1. Open this folder: `C:\Users\dell\college-hub\`
2. Double-click **`index.html`** in File Explorer.
3. It will open instantly in your web browser!

---

## 📝 How to Add or Edit Your Notes
1. Open `app.js` in any text editor (like VS Code or Notepad).
2. Look at the `collegeMaterials` list at the top.
3. You can edit the titles, subjects, descriptions, and paste your **Google Drive** or **GitHub** link in `link: "..."`.
4. To upload notes for free:
   - Upload your PDF/file to **Google Drive**.
   - Right click > **Share** > Change to **Anyone with the link can view**.
   - Copy that link and paste it into `link: "..."` in `app.js`.

---

## 🌐 How to Publish Online for FREE (in 3 Minutes)

### Option 1: GitHub Pages (Recommended)
1. Go to [GitHub.com](https://github.com) and log in.
2. Click **New Repository**, name it `college-hub`, and keep it **Public**.
3. Upload the files from this folder (`index.html`, `style.css`, `app.js`).
4. Go to repository **Settings** > **Pages** (in the left sidebar).
5. Under **Branch**, select `main` and click **Save**.
6. Within 1 minute, your site will be live at:
   `https://<your-username>.github.io/college-hub/`

### Option 2: Vercel (Drag-and-Drop or GitHub)
1. Go to [Vercel.com](https://vercel.com) and sign up with GitHub.
2. Click **Add New Project** and connect your GitHub repo (or drag-and-drop the folder).
3. Click **Deploy**.
4. You get an instant custom URL like `https://yash-college-hub.vercel.app`!
